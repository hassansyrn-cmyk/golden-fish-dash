## Encounter design

The Abyssal Octopus clears normal hazards from the arena, appears after a warning, swims in from the right, and retreats off-screen before the reward is shown. It telegraphs violet ink-bolt lanes before firing, alternates single-lane bolts with staggered two-lane patterns, and summons reef sharks. The fight preserves a readable escape route while keeping shields, extra lives, Fever Mode, and post-hit invincibility available.
