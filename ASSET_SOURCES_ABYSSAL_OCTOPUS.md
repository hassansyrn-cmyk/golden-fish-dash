## Encounter design

The Abyssal Octopus replaces the former Hammerhead encounter. The boss appears at 50 score, clears normal hazards from the arena, and remains for 22 seconds. It telegraphs violet ink-bolt lanes for one second before firing. Its sequence alternates single-lane bolts with staggered two-lane patterns. This requires deliberate vertical movement while preserving a readable safe route.

A successful survival grants 50 coins and 25 score. Shields, extra lives, Fever Mode, and the usual post-hit invincibility continue to work unchanged.
