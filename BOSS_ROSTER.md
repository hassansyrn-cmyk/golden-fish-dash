# Boss Progression Roster

Boss encounters are reserved for every 100 score. The arena enters a warning state before each encounter: normal gate spawning pauses, existing hazards are cleared, the warning overlay and alarm sound begin, and the danger-music loop starts. The boss then swims in from the right, battles, and retreats dramatically to the right before rewards are granted.

| Score | Boss | Weapons | Game-native summons | Difficulty progression |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | Abyssal Octopus | Ink lanes and cyan plasma | Jellyfish | The introductory fight has two slow jellyfish summons and wide escape lanes. |
| 200 | Electric Manta Ray | Fast electric forks and centre plasma | Sharks | Faster electric patterns add three-lane bursts; three sharks move more quickly than normal hazards. |
| 300 | Abyssal Anglerfish | Bubble traps and rapid plasma | Sea mines | Four mines combine delayed pressure with the anglerfish’s ambush pattern. |
| 400 | Ancient Leviathan | Water surges and plasma wakes | Sharks and jellyfish | Five mixed summons arrive more often, alternating shark pressure with vertical jellyfish movement. |
| 500 | Coral Kraken King | Coral spikes and warm plasma | Sharks, jellyfish, and sea mines | Seven mixed summons and the shortest interval create the final encounter’s highest pressure while keeping telegraphs visible. |

The shared rule for every encounter is that normal pipe gates and ambient hazards never overlap with boss patterns. Every weapon lane receives a visible pre-telegraph, and each sequence preserves at least one escape route. The cinematic distance transition scales only the boss and player down smoothly during the encounter; the underwater background remains rendered at full-screen size, preventing any cropped or empty edges. Imported boss artwork uses true transparency and is animated through body sway, breathing scale, bank/rotation, attack recoil, and moving energy light; it is never replaced with a code-drawn boss.
