# Boss Progression Roster

Boss encounters are reserved for every 100 score. The arena enters a warning state before each encounter: normal gate spawning pauses, existing hazards are cleared, the boss warning overlay appears, and battle music starts before the boss is drawn.

| Score | Boss | Primary weapons | Difficulty intent | Asset status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | Abyssal Octopus | Violet ink bolts, fast cyan plasma, summoned sharks | First full boss encounter; readable staggered patterns | Integrated |
| 200 | Electric Manta Ray | Electric arcs, crossing bolt lanes | Faster transitions and alternating high/low routes | Awaiting user-supplied PNG |
| 300 | Abyssal Anglerfish | Lure zones, dark bubbles, burst shots | Route deception with clear pre-telegraphs | Awaiting user-supplied PNG |
| 400 | Leviathan Sea Serpent | Water dashes, long wave lines | Sustained movement and multi-step escape routes | Awaiting user-supplied PNG |
| 500 | Coral Kraken King | Coral spikes, tentacle sweeps, reef minions | Multi-pattern finale with generous warning time | Awaiting user-supplied PNG |

The shared rule for all encounters is that the game must never combine normal pipe gates, mines, jellyfish, or ambient sharks with boss weapons. Each dangerous lane must receive a visible pre-telegraph, and every sequence must preserve an escape route.
